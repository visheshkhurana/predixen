Here’s a fresh audit of FounderConsole (based on what I’m seeing across the marketing pages, including **Features** as the “deepest” content). I’m focusing on **UI hierarchy, conversion, content clarity, and performance/structure**.

## 1) Navigation & CTA strategy (site-wide)

**What’s good**

* Clean nav items: Features / Pricing / About / Blog / FAQ.
* Clear CTAs: **Sign In** vs **Get Started Free**.

**Opportunities**

* Decide a single “primary outcome” on all marketing pages:

  * If PLG-first: primary is **Get Started Free**, secondary **Watch Demo**.
* Consider a persistent “sticky CTA strip” on mobile (one button only) to reduce friction.

**Fix**

* Make primary button visually dominant; secondary low-contrast outline.
* If `/auth` is the gate, use guardrails: “2-minute setup • Connect Stripe • baseline forecast”.

## 2) Features page audit (current state)

### 2.1 Hero / lead section

**Current**

* Headline: “Every Tool Founders Need to Survive and Scale”
* Supporting copy: positions as a full financial intelligence stack.

**Issue**

* This sounds like “full platform” but doesn’t immediately translate into “I can make a decision right now” (runway date, hiring timing, fundraising size).

**Fix**
Add a single outcome-oriented sentence right after the headline:

* “Know your runway confidence band and the tradeoffs of hiring/fundraising in minutes.”

### 2.2 Simulation Engine section

**Current**

* Strong promise: “Run thousands of Monte Carlo simulations… See P10, P50, P90.”
* Feature bullets: scenario comparison, sensitivity analysis, tornado charts, etc.

**Issues**

* “Simulation Engine” appears twice in the DOM (possibly duplicated headings/cards). That can confuse users and hurts readability/accessibility.
* All bullets are “power features”—most founders need the “why should I care?” framing.

**Fix**

* Remove duplicate headings; if it’s meant to be tabs/cards, make them visually distinct.
* Rephrase each bullet as a benefit:

  * “Tornado charts: instantly identify the top 3 risk drivers.”

### 2.3 AI Copilot section

**Current**

* Positioning: “plain English… grounded, data-backed answers… seasoned CFO.”

**Issues**

* Content claims “real-time web research.” That’s a high-stakes claim for accuracy/trust; needs a credibility anchor and boundary (sources, limitations, update cadence).
* Copilot should have explicit “audit trail” design: “why did we recommend X?” and “what assumptions changed?”

**Fix**
Add explainability UX:

* “Why this answer: #1 driver, #2 driver”
* “Data freshness: last sync”

## 3) Content balance: signal vs noise

**Issue**

* You have lots of high-value features, but the page may feel like “feature supermarket.”
* Founders decide emotionally first: “Do I trust you?” and “Will this reduce my risk?”

**Fix**

* Add 1–2 case snippets:

  * “Delay hire by 3 weeks → risk reduced from 42% to 18% (demo example)”
* Add “What’s included in 5 minutes” on Features too (not just Pricing/Home).

---

## 4) Storytelling & persuasion gaps (site-wide)

**Missing**

* Visual proof: dashboard screenshot/animation.
* “Before/After” founder narrative:

  * Before: spreadsheets, noisy board conversations.
  * After: confidence bands + explainability + consistent decision language.

**Fix**

* Add a single high-quality product preview above the fold (no stock imagery).
* Add an interactive block (Scenario Compare demo) on Home/Features to show the “aha” moment.

---

## 5) Technical/performance & crawlability

**Observed**

* The site feels heavy/slow at times. That often correlates with:

  * Large bundle, too many animations, expensive scripts, or heavy background assets.

**Fix**

* Audit bundle size & perf:

  * Lazy-load non-critical sections (blog list, footer, heavy visuals).
  * Compress images & avoid large video auto-load.
* For SEO in an SPA: prerendering marketing routes is a high-ROI step (we can integrate in your Vite config).

---

3. Ship a product preview + interactive Scenario Compare.
4. Add explainability by design: “why this recommendation” + assumptions.
