# FounderConsole — Objections & Responses

> Paste into a Google Doc titled **"KB — Objections"**. The AI agent uses this to handle pushback gracefully.

## "I just use Excel / Google Sheets"

**Acknowledge:** You can absolutely model this in a spreadsheet. Most founders do.

**Differentiate:** Spreadsheets give you a point estimate ("14 months runway"). FounderConsole runs 10,000 simulations with realistic variance on growth, burn, and timing — so instead of "we have 14 months" you get "P50 survival = 14 months, P90 survival = 8 months." Most founders are surprised by the gap.

**Low-friction CTA:** Try the free Simulator with your numbers, see if the P50/P90 gap changes how you think about your runway. Takes 5 minutes.

## "Too early to care about this / We're pre-product"

**Acknowledge:** Fair — if you have 36 months of runway and no burn, there's nothing to simulate.

**Differentiate:** The main use case pre-revenue is actually dilution modeling for your next raise. What does your cap table look like after a $2M seed at $10M vs $15M valuation? That's worth knowing before you sign a term sheet.

**CTA:** Free tier covers the runway side. Worth saving the link for when you start hiring.

## "We don't share our financials with random tools"

**Acknowledge:** Completely understandable. You shouldn't.

**Differentiate:** The free Survival Simulator runs entirely in your browser — your numbers never touch our servers. No signup, no data transmission. For Pro/Team where you want to save scenarios, data is encrypted at rest (AES-256), we're on SOC 2 track (Q4 2026), and you can delete everything with one click.

**CTA:** Try the free Simulator — zero risk, zero signup. If you like it, we can discuss data security for Pro.

## "How is this different from [Carta / Pulley / Fathom / Causal]?"

**Short version:** Carta/Pulley are cap table tools, not runway tools. Fathom/Causal are powerful but built for companies with finance teams. FounderConsole is for founders who don't have a finance team and just want fast answers.

**If they mention a specific competitor:** Genuinely engage — don't trash them. If the competitor is a better fit, say so. Long-term trust > short-term close.

## "Not fundraising right now"

**Acknowledge:** Then cap table modeling is less urgent for you.

**Differentiate:** The Survival Simulator is still useful — it tells you how far you can go without raising. Sometimes that changes the decision of "do I need to raise?"

**CTA:** Run the Simulator to see your P50 survival. If it's >18 months, you might not need to raise right now.

## "I'll come back when we're bigger"

**Respectful close:** Fair. When you're at the point of hiring your 5th person or modeling your next round, the link is founderconsole.ai. Good luck.

**Don't push harder.** This is a legitimate "not now."

## "What's the catch with the free tier?"

**Honest answer:** There isn't one. The Simulator is free forever with no signup because it's how we get founders to try us. Most founders who run a simulation once come back when they have more questions that need the Pro features (cap table, scenarios, export).

**No dark patterns.** No forced upgrades. No account creation wall.

## "Can I just build this myself?"

**Acknowledge:** Yes, with ~40 hours of work in Python/R you can build a Monte Carlo runway simulator. Some engineer founders do.

**Differentiate:** The value isn't the math — it's the ongoing effort of keeping it up to date as your numbers change, and the scenario UI for quickly comparing options. Most founders who build it stop updating it after 2 months.

**CTA:** Try the free one first. If you still want to build your own, we'll send you our variance assumptions to save you time.

## "Price is too high"

**First, clarify:** Too high vs what? Free (genuinely fine — stay free), or vs a competitor?

**If comparison to competitor:** Engage with the actual comparison. Don't bluff.

**If absolute:** $29 saves you ~2 hours of spreadsheet modeling per month at typical founder hourly rates. But if you're genuinely cash-strapped, stay on free forever — we'd rather that than lose you.

## "I don't trust AI-generated responses on financials"

**Acknowledge:** Healthy skepticism. AI has failure modes.

**Differentiate:** The Simulator isn't AI — it's Monte Carlo math you can audit. We publish our variance assumptions. The AI parts are only on the explanation side (helping you interpret what P50 means, etc.).

**CTA:** Try the Simulator, check the math, tell us if something looks off. We respond to every math-check email.

## "Send me more info"

**Don't send a PDF.** Instead: "The 5-minute live demo is faster than any PDF — founderconsole.ai/demo. If you want to book a call after that, the link is in the demo."
