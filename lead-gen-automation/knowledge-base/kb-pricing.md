## Plans

| Tier | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Any founder wanting to stress-test runway. No signup. |
| **Pro** | $29/month or $290/year | Solo / small team founders who want to save and compare scenarios, model dilution, and export for investors. |
| **Team** | $99/month or $990/year | Teams of 2–10 with multiple stakeholders — co-founders, finance contractor, board members — who need shared access and investor-ready reports. |

## What's in each tier

### Free
- Survival Simulator (runway + P50/P90 survival probability)
- Basic straight-line runway projection
- No signup required — just visit founderconsole.ai/simulator
- Browser-local computation (your numbers don't touch our servers)

### Pro ($29/mo)
Everything in Free, plus:
- Unlimited saved scenarios with names + descriptions
- Cap table modeling (seed, Series A, Series B stacking)
- Dilution preview: see post-round cap table at different valuations and round sizes
- PDF + Excel export
- Scenario comparison view (side-by-side A vs. B)
- Historical tracking — see how your numbers have changed
- Email support (<24h response)

### Team ($99/mo)
Everything in Pro, plus:
- Up to 10 user seats on the same workspace
- Investor-ready PDF reports (formatted for data rooms)
- Custom branding on exports
- API access (beta) — pull your numbers into whatever you want
- Priority support from Vishesh directly
- Quarterly strategy call with our team

## FAQs

**Is there a free trial on Pro / Team?**
Yes — 14 days, no credit card required. If you sign up through a cold email, we extend it to 30 days.

**Can I switch from monthly to annual?**
Yes, prorated credit. Annual saves 2 months (~17% discount).

**Do you have enterprise / custom pricing?**
Not yet — every customer is on Pro or Team. If your team is >10 seats or you need SOC 2 / DPA / custom SSO, email vishesh@founderconsole.ai and we'll figure something out.

**What happens to my data if I cancel?**
You can export everything to PDF/Excel before cancelling. After 90 days of inactivity, we delete your data.

**Do you offer discounts for YC, Techstars, or other accelerator companies?**
Yes — 50% off Pro for the first year. Email vishesh@founderconsole.ai with your batch info.

**Is the Survival Simulator really free forever?**
Yes. It's our front door. Runway + P50/P90 on manual inputs will always be free with no signup.

**What about a free plan with signup for more features?**
No — we deliberately split "try it (free, no signup)" vs. "save + model more (Pro)". Keeps the decision clean.

**Can I pay in [X currency]?**
Billing is USD via Stripe. Stripe auto-converts most currencies at checkout.

**Do you take ACH / invoicing?**
Yes on Team for annual plans. Email us.

## Common price objections + answers

- *"$29 is too much for a pre-seed founder"* → Try the free Simulator first. If it doesn't save you 2 hours of spreadsheet modeling this month, don't upgrade.
- *"Too early to need cap table modeling"* → Probably true. Stay on Free until your first priced round is 3 months out. Then upgrade for a week to model scenarios and downgrade after.
- *"We already use [Fathom / Causal]"* → Those are great — probably overkill for <10 people. Stay on theirs if it works. Ours is faster for founder-specific questions.
